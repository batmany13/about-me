# Blog candidates

A running tally of topics that could grow into longer posts — the start of a Substack, if it happens.  Each one comes out of a week's reflection; after every weekly I ask myself whether that week added a topic, or more to one already here.  The candidates aren't written yet; what's been published is listed under Published.

| Topic | What it would cover | Seeded in |
|---|---|---|
| **The outcome model** | How I structure work as outcomes with numbered steps, and why: iteration is the point, each step gives discovery somewhere to land, follow-ons keep an outcome finishable, and plans grew into named blocks with their own steps.  What closing an outcome well takes, and where the structure still leaks focus. | [W37](2026-W37.md), [W38](2026-W38.md), [W39](2026-W39.md), [W40](2026-W40.md) |
| **Positioning before pricing** | Expanding the W40 Top of Mind: before pricing, sales motion or the next feature, what is at the core of what you offer?  Can you say it in a few bullet points, and does each capability build on that core?  The vision, and the step you're taking toward it — not the fancy site. | [W40](2026-W40.md), [W43](2026-W43.md) |
| **Learnings for today's age** (intro) | Starting with my own experience: what nearly seven years at Netflix taught me about leading engineering teams, applied to how teams and companies work now that agents do much of the building.  The opening post for the series below. | [W41](2026-W41.md) |
| **What does leadership mean now?** (a series) | Added every so often, from what I'm seeing in conversations with founders, the engineering circle and other leaders: each post turns a set of discussions into one thought.  A pool of topics to add to and iterate on, starting with **aligning people and agents** and **outcome-driven development**. | [W41](2026-W41.md) |
| **From vibe to agentic engineering** | Leadership and tech from the seat of a solo builder: catching up on ten years of how software gets built, and moving from vibe coding, to a harness, to agentic engineering and architecture.  What changes about how you lead, and what you lead, when the team is mostly agents. | [W42](2026-W42.md) |
| **What is good for what?** | Going deeper on comparing agent frameworks and models hands on, and on how you'd even decide which one fits which job, for the coding and for the product work.  Seeded by this week's building learning and the typed decision models in Interesting Tech. | [W42](2026-W42.md) |
