## Top of Mind for Founders & Leaders

<!-- fnr:top_of_mind -->
Balancing Cloud and On-premise work.  Each requires thoughtfulness.  For Cloud, what are your key 1-2 growth metrics (e.g. DAU, retention), and then what are the levers you can use to move this?  Do you have an experimentation iteration to show it working?  For on-premise, this is a mix of contract complexity, deployment method, appropriate pricing and environment compatibility.  Some of these only come from POCs and early on, just have to learn by doing.

As a startup founder, you are sprinting constantly, but taking even short breaks, and letting your mind wander, can bring clarity to tough and challenging problems.  I know it feels like you can't stop, but think of it as a way to sharpen your mental acuity.  Don't forget sleep is important too!

## Interesting Tech

<!-- fnr:tech -->
- **[Redesigning source control at Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control)** (Anthropic Engineering, Oct 13) — Anthropic built Scion with 7 engineers, and migrated off git in five months while daily merges grew 10x+ from 2.5k / day in May to 30k in Sept.  This bar is very high, and it even mentions in the article that many others can't just spend the same, and they have a very specific need.  So, it's a good example of learning from the frontier labs, but this is a very problem-driven solution, and most won't even come close to this level of scale, so how do you apply the learnings for your environment?  What do you take away?
- **Typed decision models** — not new, but using them hands on shows how they augment an agent rather than replace it.  A small model takes application state plus a typed question and returns a calibrated answer, called from code like a function instead of prompted like a chat model.  [TypeSafe's Jev](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction) documents three primitives: **Choice** (pick one option from a set, with a probability for each), **Score** (rate against ordered levels) and **Noul** (the probability that a yes/no statement is true).  Applied: Choice for routing and categorization, Score for grading against a rubric, and Noul as a gate, so the expensive model only runs when confidence is low.  Open alternatives now exist: [Bespoke's Nimble](https://docs.bespokelabs.ai/nimble) and [CLM-8B](https://huggingface.co/Contrastive-LM/CLM-v0.1-8B).
- **[AI Agents Push Humans Out of the Loop](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2608.23642)** (Mitchell, Ghosh and Passi, Sep 2026) — a position paper that pairs with [last week's](2026-W41.md) comprehension audits: "human in the loop" is not the same as meaningful oversight, and an approval prompt doesn't mean anyone evaluated what they approved.  Its answer is to support the person, not just the agent: commit to your own judgment before seeing the agent's, gate the consequential steps, review in logical batches, and protect the reviewer's skill with practice and breaks.  Supporting scrutiny in the moment is a good lesson for my building, and for anyone running agents.  It is an agenda drawn from prior research rather than new experiments.
