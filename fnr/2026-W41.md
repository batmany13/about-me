<!-- /fnr:fund -->

**Learning:**  <!-- fnr:fund_learning -->
Aligning people now means aligning their agents, and the problem is different because context has to be shared at agentic speed.  Underneath it is a common problem of coordination.  The techniques I heard this week:
- **A separate context repo.**  Onboarding lives there, and it's where every engineer, and every engineer's agents, starts.
- **An agentic "chief of staff"** that coordinates other, narrower chiefs of staff.
- **Humans swarm on what's plaguing the agents.**  An engineering team spikes together on a faulty area, so the agents can be aligned on the work.
- **Every IC is their own manager.**  Not a new idea, but it was reinforced: how to lead agents is the modern consideration.

Human judgement and sharing context still matter.  The question: what business or product context is shared, how well understood is it, and then how does that translate to the agentic work?
