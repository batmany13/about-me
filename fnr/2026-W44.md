## Top of Mind for Founders & Leaders

<!-- fnr:top_of_mind -->
**Change management with larger teams.**  Landing a message takes a lot of work, thought and care.  Similar to software, you may want to iterate on a smaller batch first, see how it works, and then expand more broadly (of course, this isn't possible for all changes).  Make sure to have advocates who will champion your idea!

**Positioning: different needs at different stages.**

- **Initial customer use cases may lead you to the actual product discovery.**  Finding those first few customers where your message resonates, and be careful how much effort you have to put in.  If you find a more interesting wedge, it may be best to pursue that.
- **Ease of discovery of value.**  Make it easy for humans and agents to discover the use cases, samples, examples of how to use your product, and use metrics to guide you on friction points to tweak
- **Show the complexity.**  In the AI era everyone thinks they can build everything themselves, but a prototype is not the real thing, so how can you "show" the complexity in the scaling, operations and maintainability of a solution.  Position papers, thought-leadership pieces and use-case deep dives make that difficulty visible.  If you have OSS, use it to show what your tool does and the hard problems it solves.
<!-- /fnr:top_of_mind -->

## Interesting Tech

<!-- fnr:tech -->
- **RL environments, and who has opened what** — a small sampling of the frameworks, trainers and harnesses behind them, with their licenses:
  - **Open frameworks:** [OpenEnv](https://github.com/openenv/OpenEnv) (BSD-3-Clause; launched by Meta-PyTorch and Hugging Face, hosted by the Linux Foundation; a reset/step/state contract with MCP tools), [Harbor](https://github.com/harbor-framework/harbor) (Apache-2.0; the Terminal-Bench creators' task format and test-script verifier, run on Docker or about 30 cloud sandboxes), [NeMo Gym](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/Gym) (NVIDIA's open library to build, run and score environments) and [SkyRL](https://github.com/NovaSky-AI/SkyRL) (an open trainer with code-defined rewards).
  - **Open pieces from platforms and suppliers:** Prime Intellect's [verifiers](https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/verifiers) (MIT) and [prime-rl](https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/prime-rl) (Apache-2.0), with the Environments Hub, Lab and Sandboxes closed; Mercor's [Archipelago](https://github.com/Mercor-Intelligence/archipelago) harness (Apache-2.0) and an [APEX subset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mercor/apex-agents); Surge AI's [DAYJOB](https://github.com/surge-ai/dayjob) harness (Apache-2.0); Bespoke Labs' [Curator](https://github.com/bespokelabsai/curator) and [Sandbox](https://github.com/bespokelabsai/sandbox) (both Apache-2.0), with its environment engine closed; and the Open Reward Standard SDK from [General Reasoning](https://openreward.ai) (Apache-2.0, with the registry closed).
  - **Closed:** the environments Mercor and Surge AI sell are built to order and not public, [Fleet](https://fleetai.com) keeps its hosted replicas of enterprise software closed behind an Apache-2.0 SDK, and [Halluminate](https://halluminate.ai) and [Mechanize](https://mechanize.work) sell finance and coding environments to labs with no verifier code to read.

## What I've Been Learning

<!-- fnr:learning -->
Read a book on RL environments, which covers both models and agents.  It follows the history from the early days of Gym, where an environment was a simple task for training a model with RL, to today's much more complicated testing and training of agents, which need stateful worlds that reset.  Gained a deeper appreciation of the space, how the tech and market is evolving.  Immediately useful as talked about it with two founders who are considering entering that space.

- **The pieces:** task, world or sandbox, harness, verifier and reward, rollout infrastructure, and trainer.
- **The verifier stands out.**  The reward is only a proxy for success, so the verifier decides what counts, and it should be kept separate from the world.  If the agent can see or change the tests it's graded by, it can pass them without doing the task, so restore the tests or withhold them until scoring.

Ep. 5 of NFX's Masterclass, [Magic Words](https://youtu.be/glmAg6IMPLs), is good, it's the power of language.  What's really moved my own personal work on building started after settling on a name for the product, and gearing everything towards that vision.  The product offering shifted, but the name and "manifesto" has sharpened.  The framing in it: language -> figure out how to market it, channels etc, then product.  Which is such an interesting way to think about it.
