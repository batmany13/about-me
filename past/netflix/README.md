## What I took with me

The three things I'd carry into any org, in rough order of how hard they were to learn:

- **Context over control.**  The freedom-and-responsibility model only works if context propagates faster than decisions need to be made.  Most "alignment" problems are actually context-latency problems.
- **Culture is pivot capacity.**  Netflix pivoted DVD→streaming, licensing→originals, US→global, and is now running Live, Games, Ads, and Podcasts.  Culture wasn't why those bets were smart; it was why the org could execute them without its own immune system killing them first.  More on this in [thinking with Claude](../../ideas/thinking_with_claude.md).
- **The product has to work first.**  Culture multiplies whatever the business already is.  It doesn't substitute for product-market fit.  I had this backwards for years — see [four laws](../../ideas/four_laws.md).
